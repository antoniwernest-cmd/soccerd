TEACHER GUIDE — WORLD CUP WORDS

Teacher audit/preview PIN: 259. Preview is separate from saved student work.
The landing page uses a picture-only ID picker with 20 choices. Each picture/word-set combination has its own saved record; there is no typed name/code field.
Use A− / A+ for supported text sizes 18, 20, 22 and 24. Voice directions are optional. Slow .8 is the default; Extra slow .6 and Steady 1 are available. Recorded voice rate is -4. Phonemes are separated by 1.2-second pauses and normalized so quiet sounds remain audible.

INTERACTION AND FEEDBACK
Drag words or letter tiles onto their visible landing pads. The actual tiles stay on the pads, can move again, and return to the bank. Keyboard/tap uses the same pads. Wrong answers are retained for review; the cloze checkmark appears only on an exact correct placement and disappears if the tile moves away. Reduced motion shows the final check without spinning.
The 3-view scene includes flags, a balloon above the field, a TV crew, soccer parachute, The Big Bear mascot, soccer-ball bottle bowling, and the dog chasing a soccer ball. Other pictures show warm-ups, dog tricks, the rainy day, team picture, airport and soccer on a boat. The supplied skipping-rope artwork is retained as explicitly approved.
Sharing dropdowns cannot allocate more than the remaining pool plus that player's current allocation. All 9 balls remain visible in the pool or beside players. Correct division requires equal groups of 3 and answer 3. Multiplication answer is 9.
Truck boxes show capacity on their front side. Same-capacity boxes form counted stacks to keep any supported load on one screen. Each drag/remove moves one actual box. A truck lights while its load is exact; honks only when changing to the exact load, not when reopening the screen. The report records every box move and final quantity.

VOICE UPDATE — NOVEMBER 21
Each word is spoken once per requested play. Sound buttons say the word once, then its separated sounds once, with no spoken preamble or repeated final word. Placement praise/prompts are visual only. Repeated taps during one recording count immediately without restarting or queuing it. Replay is available after the recording ends. Each cloze headphone reads only its own sentence once; there is no automatic passage playback.

PICTURE BUTTONS AND STAGGERED BOXES — NOVEMBER 21
Every sentence button on both listening pages has a matching descriptive soccer illustration. Selecting a sentence enlarges its button and picture within the activity while the sentence is read once. Playback completion, Stop, an audio error, or navigation returns it to the normal grid; voice directions and Stop remain visible.
Loaded boxes are visibly offset in staggered stacks. Up to ten actual box pictures are shown in each stack, with the full count badge retained for larger loads. Dragging moves one box at a time, shown as one box under the pointer. Quantities, capacities, totals and saved work are retained.
