# Soccer sentence audit
Each blank has one target in its displayed bank. Full audio states the target word.

- set1: The goal's woven mesh is a ___. **net** — Woven mesh distinguishes the goal net from other equipment.
- set1: The winner lifts the gold prize ___. **cup** — The gold prize cup, not a kit container.
- set1: A shirt and shorts together make a ___. **kit** — An entire outfit, rather than a container or separate bib.
- set1: A ___ has a brim above the player's eyes. **cap** — Only cap is headwear with a brim.
- set1: A ___ cheers for the team from the stands. **fan** — Only fan names a human supporter in this bank.
- set1: The zipped ___ holds the team's kit. **bag** — The zipped container, rather than the outfit inside it.
- set1: The mesh training vest is called a ___. **bib** — A sleeveless training vest, distinct from a whole kit.
- set1: The keeper's foam knee protector is a ___. **pad** — Foam protection rather than clothing or a container.
- set1: The team flies to the cup in a passenger ___. **jet** — The only aircraft word in the bank.
- set1: Kick the ball to ___ the bottle targets. **hit** — Soccer-ball bowling. No competing contact verb in this bank.
- set2: The TV crew drives a broadcast ___. **van** — A wheeled road vehicle, rather than another item.
- set2: Keep your bottom on the bench: ___. **sit** — Seated position rather than another movement.
- set2: Move fast on alternate feet: ___. **run** — Alternate feet excludes hopping.
- set2: Keep one foot up and ___ on the other. **hop** — Only one foot is used repeatedly.
- set2: A light touch with your boot is a ___. **tap** — A gentle boot touch; hit is in the other bank.
- set2: The body part from hip to foot is a ___. **leg** — The only body part in this bank.
- set2: Wet dirt on the soccer pitch is ___. **mud** — The substance is wet dirt, not a cloth.
- set2: A torn cleaning cloth is a ___. **rag** — Torn cleaning material rather than a wearable label.
- set2: Two rows of teeth close the bag with a ___. **zip** — The zipper mechanism, not the bag itself.
- set2: The pet that barks is a ___. **dog** — The only animal noun in this bank.
