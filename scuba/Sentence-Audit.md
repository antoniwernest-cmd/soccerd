# Scuba sentence audit
Each independent clue identifies one answer in its displayed bank.

- Dive gear: The long rubber diving flipper is a ___. **fin** — A separate manufactured diving flipper, not the whole cod fish.
- Dive gear: The small underwater vehicle is a ___. **sub** — Only sub is an underwater vehicle; other transport is in the other bank.
- Dive gear: The fish with a chin barbel is a ___. **cod** — The only whole fish in this bank; fin is a rubber diving flipper.
- Dive gear: The cleanup scoop made of mesh is a ___. **net** — Only net has mesh for scooping loose litter.
- Dive gear: Rinse the dive gear in the round water ___. **tub** — A large round rinse container, unlike a cup or a zipped bag.
- Dive gear: The zipped dive-gear container is a ___. **bag** — A zipped fabric luggage container, not a tub or drinking cup.
- Dive gear: Drink water from the small handleless ___. **cup** — The small handleless drinking vessel, unlike the large rinse tub.
- Dive gear: The paper plan of the reef route is a ___. **map** — Only map is a paper route plan.
- Dive gear: The boat headwear with a brim is a ___. **cap** — Only cap is brimmed headwear.
- Dive gear: The wooden pinch fastener on the line is a ___. **peg** — A wooden clothespeg, distinct from the other large gear objects.
- Dive day: The gear covered in water drops is ___. **wet** — Only wet is an adjective describing water on equipment; dip is a quick dunk.
- Dive day: The bright daytime star is the ___. **sun** — Only sun names the daytime star.
- Dive day: Put shore litter in the large lidded ___. **bin** — Only bin is the large waste container.
- Dive day: The small name label on the gear is a ___. **tag** — Only tag is a small attached identification label.
- Dive day: Two rows of bag teeth close with a ___. **zip** — The toothed fastener, unlike the attached label tag.
- Dive day: All the diving equipment forms a ___. **kit** — A complete collection of gear, unlike one label or fastener.
- Dive day: The team flies in the passenger ___. **jet** — The only aircraft in this bank; van is a road vehicle.
- Dive day: The dive team drives the road ___. **van** — The only wheeled road vehicle in this bank.
- Dive day: A quick dunk in the water is a ___. **dip** — Dip is the only noun for a quick dunk; wet is a state.
- Dive day: Rest your bottom on the boat bench: ___. **sit** — Only sit describes the seated position.
