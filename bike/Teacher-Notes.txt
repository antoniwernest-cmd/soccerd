CITY BIKE RIDE — WORD ADVENTURE

Open index.html in this activity folder, or use the shared ../index.html landing page.
This is an uncompressed, buildless HTML activity. Keep all relative files and assets in their folders. No ZIP is required.

TWO WORD SETS: Ride start; Park stop.
Choose one of 20 picture-only IDs. Each picture/set saves separately in this bike activity; all four adventures have separate storage and lesson IDs. The Adventures link returns to the shared landing page.

18 SCREENS, FOUR PARTS
Words and sounds: three immediate taps per word, first letters, initial consonants, short vowels, final letters and two beginning-blend pages. Letter/blend trays are below the word gaps.
Match and share: three ten-picture matches; three eight-label scene views (Home start, City path, Park stop); sharing nine bike bells among three riders with division/multiplication; load trucks with boxes of 1, 5, 10 and 100 bike bells to total 28 and 127.
Sentences: two ten-sentence pages. Every button shows a matching picture, enlarges while its selected sentence is read once, and returns when playback ends or Stop is pressed.
Fill gaps: three ten-gap listening pages. Click a sentence's own headphones to hear it once, then playback stops. There is no whole-passage button, automatic next sentence or automatic spoken placement praise. Correct cloze answers show a glowing checkmark; moving the answer removes it. Reduced motion shows the check without animation.

Drag tiles to their visible pads. Keyboard/tap uses the same pads. Placed tiles can move, swap and return; placements persist between pages. Staggered box stacks show their capacities and counts; dragging moves one box. Exact truck loads light up and give a short honk once when becoming correct.
Directions are optional. Slow is the default, with Extra slow and Steady choices. Recording taps count immediately without queuing or restarting the active recording. Full listens are recorded separately. Changing pages stops playback.

TEACHER PREVIEW: PIN 259, either set, any screen or report. Preview work is separate from student work.
Accuracy maximum: 123 = 20 core matching/cloze + 35 letter/sound items + 40 blend matching/cloze + 24 scene labels + 2 maths questions + 2 truck targets. Listening counts are separate. Save full report PDF exports 10 itemized pages.
