# City Bike Ride sentence audit
Each independent clue identifies one answer in its displayed bank.

- Ride start: The large city vehicle has over twenty seats: a ___. **bus** — A large public passenger vehicle distinguishes bus from delivery van and taxi.
- Ride start: Sliding doors open on the delivery ___. **van** — Delivery use and sliding side door identify the van.
- Ride start: The taxi driver drives a yellow ___. **cab** — Taxi is the specific synonym for cab.
- Ride start: The paper drawing shows our bike route: a ___. **map** — A route drawn on paper names the map, not a bag or repair kit.
- Ride start: The zipped sack on the bike rack is a ___. **bag** — The zipped container is distinct from the tools of the kit inside it.
- Ride start: The cloth hat with a front brim is a ___. **cap** — Cloth brimmed headwear identifies cap.
- Ride start: The body part from hip to foot is a ___. **leg** — The only named body part in this bank.
- Ride start: The short axle footrest is a bike ___. **peg** — Cylindrical axle footrest distinguishes peg from the hexagonal nut.
- Ride start: The six-sided metal fastener is a ___. **nut** — Hexagonal threaded hardware distinguishes nut from the long footpeg.
- Ride start: The set of bike repair tools is a ___. **kit** — A tool set rather than the container holding it.
- Park stop: Park litter goes in the rubbish ___. **bin** — The rigid park waste container is the bin.
- Park stop: Turn the handle of the water ___. **tap** — A handled drinking-water outlet identifies the tap.
- Park stop: The leashed pet that barks is a ___. **dog** — Barking identifies the dog, distinct from the web-spinning creature.
- Park stop: The bright round star lights the day: the ___. **sun** — The daytime star, not a coloured lamp or object.
- Park stop: The top stop light is ___. **red** — The only colour word in this bank.
- Park stop: Two rows of teeth close the bag with a ___. **zip** — The toothed fastener identifies zip.
- Park stop: The handleless drinking vessel is a ___. **cup** — A small open drinking container, with no competing vessel in this bank.
- Park stop: Wipe your shoes on the woven ___. **mat** — The floor covering used for shoes identifies the mat.
- Park stop: The queue of stuck cars is a traffic ___. **jam** — Traffic makes jam a road queue rather than a food.
- Park stop: The spider spins a silk ___. **web** — Silk woven by a spider identifies the web.
