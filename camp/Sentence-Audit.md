# Summer Camp sentence audit
Each independent clue identifies one answer in its displayed bank.

- Campsite: The small wooden shelter with a roof is a ___. **hut** — Only hut names the roofed wooden shelter.
- Campsite: A cut piece of tree trunk is a ___. **log** — A solid cut trunk, unlike a thin twig or a building.
- Campsite: The tiny six-legged insect is a ___. **bug** — The only insect noun in this bank.
- Campsite: A small handled drinking vessel is a ___. **mug** — Small drinking vessel with a handle; pot is deep cooking equipment.
- Campsite: The paper plan of the camp paths is a ___. **map** — The paper route plan, unlike equipment.
- Campsite: The headwear with a brim is a ___. **cap** — Only cap is headwear.
- Campsite: The zipped container for camp clothes is a ___. **bag** — A zipped luggage container rather than a cooking/drinking vessel.
- Campsite: The insect catcher made of mesh is a ___. **net** — Only net has mesh for catching insects.
- Campsite: Soup cooks in the deep two-handled ___. **pot** — Deep cooking vessel with two side handles; pan is shallow.
- Campsite: The shallow fryer with a long handle is a ___. **pan** — Shallow frying vessel with one long handle, unlike pot or mug.
- Cabin and lake: A tent rope hooks onto a ground ___. **peg** — A ground stake, not a fishing rod or a fastener zip.
- Cabin and lake: A pillow and blanket rest on this ___. **bed** — Only bed is furniture for sleeping.
- Cabin and lake: The small handleless drinking vessel is a ___. **cup** — Handleless drinking vessel; jug has a large handle and pouring spout.
- Cabin and lake: Put camp rubbish into the large lidded ___. **bin** — Large rubbish container, not a drinking vessel.
- Cabin and lake: Wipe your boots on the flat ___. **mat** — A boot-wiping floor pad, unlike bed furniture.
- Cabin and lake: The long fishing pole is a ___. **rod** — A complete fishing pole, unlike the short tent peg.
- Cabin and lake: The sticky fruit spread in the jar is ___. **jam** — Only jam is a sticky food spread.
- Cabin and lake: Pour water from the large spouted ___. **jug** — Large pouring vessel with a spout and handle, unlike handleless cup.
- Cabin and lake: The camp pet that barks is a ___. **dog** — Only dog is a barking animal.
- Cabin and lake: Two rows of teeth close the bag with a ___. **zip** — The toothed fastener, rather than a container or ground stake.
